# Hyphenation patterns

The patterns markout hyphenates by, from the
[hyph-utf8](https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen) package of the TeX
community, as its plain-text files: `hyph-*.pat.txt` holds the patterns of a
language, `hyph-*.hyp.txt` the words that are exceptions to them. They are
not changed.

Each set has its own authors and licence. `hyph-*.NOTICE.txt` is the head of
the file the set comes from, with its copyright notice and licence, as it is
there.

| Files | Language | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `hyph-hu` | Hungarian | MPL 1.1, GPL 2.0 or LGPL 2.1, at the user's choice; markout takes it under the LGPL 2.1 |
| `hyph-en-us` | English, American spelling | free to copy and distribute with its notice kept |
| `hyph-en-gb` | English, British spelling | MIT |
